Project Scope

Four Mediums. One Mission.

Literature

Novels, memoirs, poetry collections, and essay anthologies. Published in paperback and e-book through our cooperative press.

Film

Short films, documentaries, and screenplay adaptations. Produced through partnerships with independent filmmakers.

Journalism

Investigative reporting, opinion columns, and feature stories published in The Phuckin' Wire, our quarterly newspaper.

Stage & Visual Art

Paintings, mixed media, theatrical scripts, and performance pieces exhibited in galleries and community venues.

How It Works

From Submission to Revenue

1
SUBMISSION

Submit Your Story

Incarcerated creators submit manuscripts, screenplays, visual art, or journalism through our mail-in or facility partnership channels.

2
PAIRING

Meet Your Partner

Each accepted creator is paired with a skilled outside volunteer — an editor, designer, producer, or legal advisor — through our structured pairing program.

3
DEVELOPMENT

Refine the Work

Creator-volunteer pairs collaborate through mail, facility visits, and our coordination team to develop the work to publication standard.

4
PRODUCTION

Bring It to Life

Zo Media handles design, printing, distribution, and marketing. Film projects enter post-production with our partner studios.

5
REVENUE

Earn What You Create

Creators retain 50% of all revenue. The other 50% funds operations, new projects, and the cooperative infrastructure that makes it all possible.
