  Isabella is an MA Cultural Heritage Studies student at University College London (UCL). In 2024 she graduated from Stony Brook University with a BA in Cultural Anthropology.   For over six years, Isabella has volunteered for organizations within her community. She joined the UBFSF Media & Marketing team in October 2023 and helps facilitate the creation and administration of the organizational newsletter and media campaigns. She currently serves as Media & Marketing Program Director.
