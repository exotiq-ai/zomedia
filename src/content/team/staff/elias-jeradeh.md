  Elias Jeradeh completed a Master's in Biology in Lebanon, focusing on immunological mechanisms at the heart of atherosclerosis and cardiovascular disease. In alignment with those interests, he decided to pursue a Master's in Public Health (MPH) at the University of Central Oklahoma (UCO), where he became acquainted with qualitative studies and learned to think beyond the scope of numerical statistics.

  With the help of his advisor, he began researching the various determinants and detriments of incarceration. He is grateful and excited to volunteer for UBFSF as our Program & Policy Coordinator, joining the quest to investigate, address, and offer solutions to emerging public health issues.
