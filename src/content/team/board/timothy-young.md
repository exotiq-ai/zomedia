bio: |
  Timothy D. Young, M.Ed., is a dedicated educator, entrepreneur, and community leader with over 30 years of experience. A proud U.S. Army veteran who served through ROTC and the Oklahoma National Guard, he holds a Master's degree in Public-School Administration and a Bachelor of Arts in Criminal Justice, with a minor in Military Science.

  Throughout his distinguished career in education, Young has served as a Principal, Assistant Principal, Athletic Director, and Coach. His influence extends into entrepreneurship as the founder of A Heavenly Taste, LLC, overseeing ventures such as Kinyon's Elite Sauce and Young's Gourmet Sweets. He also serves as the Executive Director of the Dunbar Heights Community Group and is a former City Council member.

  An accomplished author of "Pathway to Trauma Recovery," Young is also a certified football and basketball official with over 21 years of experience at the high school, college, and professional levels. His lifelong commitment to service has earned him induction into the Shawnee Football Hall of Fame and the 2022 Shawnee Chamber of Commerce Community Service Award.
