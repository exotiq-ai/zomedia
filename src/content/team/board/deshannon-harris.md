bio: |
  DeShannon (De) Harris is a Certified Executive Coach, Certified Leadership Coach, Gallup-Certified Strengths Coach, Emergenetics Certified Associate, and Certified Coach of Excellence. She holds a Master's in Business Administration and is pursuing a Doctorate in Business Administration, Leadership. Her doctoral research centers on organizational leadership behavior and employee engagement.

  De is an accomplished Organizational Development Professional for the Tulsa County Health Department in Tulsa, Oklahoma, where she creates leadership development content and facilitates training sessions for a 75-member leadership team. Throughout her career, she has spearheaded learning and development initiatives that have impacted over 5,000 employees, designing transformative onboarding and orientation programs that establish a strong foundation for employee success.

  De's dedication to inclusive, purpose-driven leadership has led her to collaborate with UBFSF Founder Ivan Kilgore, developing leadership training initiatives for incarcerated individuals. This work reflects her belief in second chances and her commitment to equipping others with the skills necessary for successful reintegration and lifelong growth.
