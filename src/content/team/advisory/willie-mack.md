bio: |
  Willie Mack is a Ph.D. candidate at Stony Brook University and a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Black Studies Department at the University of Missouri-Columbia. His research interests focus on race, capitalism, and the carceral state in twentieth-century United States. His dissertation takes a transnational approach to the development of the carceral state in Haiti and the U.S. from the 1970s through the 1990s.

  Will has published with Black Perspectives, the blog for the African American Intellectual History Society; the Society for U.S. Intellectual History; and "Next Chapter," the digital forum for the University of Chicago's Race and Capitalism Project. He won the Organization of American Historians' 2022 John Higham Research Fellowship Award for graduate students writing a doctoral dissertation in American history.
