Get Involved

Whether you're inside or outside the walls, there's a role for you in the Zo Media cooperative. We need creators, volunteers, and supporters.

For Incarcerated Creators

Contribute Your Work

If you're incarcerated and have a manuscript, poetry collection, visual art, screenplay, or journalism to share, Zo Media wants to hear from you. We accept submissions by mail and through facility partnerships.

  • Manuscripts (any stage — draft to polished)
  • Poetry collections or individual poems
  • Visual art (photographs of originals accepted)
  • Screenplays and film treatments
  • Journalism, essays, and opinion pieces for The Wire
Submit Your Work
For Students

10-Week Pairing Program

We partner with universities and professional programs to offer structured 10-week internships. Students are paired with an incarcerated creator and co-manage a real publishing project from start to finish.

  • Editorial — copyediting, developmental editing, proofreading
  • Design — book covers, interior layout, marketing materials
  • Marketing — social media, distribution strategy, press outreach
  • Legal — contract review, IP rights management, rights licensing
  • Film — post-production, festival submissions, distribution
Apply as Intern
For Professionals

Volunteer Your Expertise

If you're a working professional in publishing, law, film, design, or marketing, your skills can directly change someone's life. Our volunteer program pairs you with an incarcerated creator for a structured collaboration.

  • Flexible time commitment (5-10 hours/month typical)
  • Structured onboarding and support from Zo Media staff
  • Meaningful portfolio work with real impact
  • Community of like-minded professionals
  • Letters of recommendation and references available
