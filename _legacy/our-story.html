About Us

Our Story

Zo Media Productions is a media cooperative that publishes literature, film, and visual art created by incarcerated people. We exist because the stories inside America's prisons are as urgent — and as universal — as any being told outside them.

The Beginning

How It Started

Ivan Kilgore, an author serving a life sentence in California, founded Zo Media Productions while incarcerated. After self-publishing Domestic Genocide: The Institutionalization of Society — a work that exposed the systemic dysfunction of the American prison system — he realized that the problem was not a lack of talent behind bars. It was a lack of infrastructure. Writers, artists, filmmakers, and thinkers inside the system had no reliable way to produce, publish, and distribute their work to the outside world.

That realization became the seed of something larger. Kilgore envisioned a cooperative — not a charity, not a nonprofit program, but a real business built from the inside out. A structure where incarcerated creators retain ownership of their intellectual property, earn revenue from their work, and develop professional skills through structured partnerships with outside volunteers. Zo Media Productions was born from that vision.

The name "Zo" comes from the term incarcerated people use for prison — "the Zo," short for "the Twilight Zone" — a reference to the disorientation and dysfunction of life inside. By claiming that name, Zo Media turns a term of confinement into one of creative power. The stories that emerge from the Zo are not pleas for sympathy. They are literature, journalism, art, and film that stand on their own merit — and that happen to come from a place most Americans will never see.

"Every person has a story worth telling. We built Zo Media to make sure those stories reach the world."

Ivan Kilgore Founder & CEO, Zo Media Productions
How We Work

The Cooperative Model

Zo Media is not a traditional publisher. It is a cooperative — meaning the people who create the work share in the ownership and revenue it generates.

Our model is built on a 50/50 revenue split. Creators retain 50% of all net revenue from their work — books, art, film, and newsletter content. The other 50% funds cooperative operations, new projects, and the infrastructure that makes production possible from inside prison walls.

This is not charity. This is economic participation. Every incarcerated creator who publishes through Zo Media is building a professional body of work, earning real income, and contributing to a cooperative that grows stronger with every title published.

Collaboration

The Pairing Model

At the core of every Zo Media project is a partnership. Every project pairs an incarcerated creator — the Inside Partner — with a free-world volunteer — the Outside Partner. The inside voice drives the creative vision. The outside partner handles the digital logistics. Both share equal authority and responsibility.

Role Inside Partner Outside Partner
Editorial Author, Poet, Essayist Editor, Writing Coach
Design Art Direction, Cover Concepts Graphic Designer, Typesetter
Marketing Strategy Input, Audience Insight Digital Marketing, Distribution
Legal Rights Holder, IP Owner Paralegal, Contract Review
Film Screenwriter, Director of Vision Producer, Post-Production

This is not volunteerism in the traditional sense. It is collaboration between equals building something real. The pairing model ensures that no project moves forward without the creator's voice at the center.
