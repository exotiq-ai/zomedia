How We Work

Every Role,
Two Voices

At the core of Zo Media is the Pairing Model. Every position in our cooperative — from Managing Editor to Art Director — is co-held by one incarcerated member and one outside volunteer.

The inside voice drives the creative vision. The outside partner handles digital logistics. Both share equal authority. This is not charity work. It is collaboration between equals building something real.
