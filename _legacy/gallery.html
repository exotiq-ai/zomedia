Gallery Partnership Model

Art created inside prison walls deserves to be seen, collected, and valued. Our gallery partnership model ensures that every sale benefits the artist first.

Our gallery partnership model splits revenue three ways: 40% to the artist, 30% to the gallery or exhibition partner, and 30% to Zo Media for operations and new projects.
