The Phuckin' Wire

Journalism from the Inside — Uncensored, Unfiltered, Unflinching

The Wire is a quarterly newspaper written entirely by incarcerated journalists. It covers prison reform, legal analysis, investigative reporting, creative writing, and the stories that mainstream media refuses to tell.

What You'll Get

Four Sections. Four Perspectives.

Section One

Investigative Journalism

Long-form investigations into prison conditions, policy failures, and systemic abuse — reported by the people who live it. Every story is fact-checked, sourced, and edited to professional standards.

Section Two

Legal Analysis

Case law breakdowns, sentencing reform updates, and rights-based reporting written by incarcerated legal scholars. The Wire tracks legislation that affects 2.3 million Americans behind bars.

Section Three

Creative Writing

Poetry, fiction excerpts, memoir fragments, and visual art. The Wire's creative section showcases the raw literary talent developing inside Zo Media's cooperative.

Section Four

Opinion & Editorial

First-person perspectives on justice, family, identity, and survival. Unfiltered voices from people the system was designed to silence.
